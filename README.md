# urbooth — Cute Cartoon & Rock Band Photobooth Studio

Interactive photobooth web application featuring the elegant handwritten **Handflair** signature font, adorable cartoon doodles (bunnies, cats, bears, dinos), and authentic rock band concert strips.

---

## ✨ What's New & Features

### 1. Identity & Handflair Signature Typography
- **Name**: **`urbooth`**
- **Handflair Font**: Fluid, expressive handwriting signature penmanship applied to branding, photostrip footer imprints, cut corner marks (`♡ 01`), and live UI headers.
- **Warm & Playful Palette**: Soft pastel blushes, warm butter cream, stage obsidian, and acid lavender.

### 2. Cartoon Doodles & Rock Band Frame Collections
- **🐾 Cute Cartoon Frames**:
  - `Bunny & Smiling Stars (1x4 Strip)`: Soft blush pink with hand-drawn floppy bunnies, smiling stars, floating hearts, and "besties forever ♡".
  - `Cat Café & Paws (1x3 Strip)`: Butter cream with sleeping cartoon kittens, paw prints, fish bones, and cat doodles.
  - `Teddy Bear Bakery (2x2 Grid)`: Warm latte tone with cafe teddy bears, toasted croissants, and bakery stickers.
  - `Happy Dino & Clouds (1x4 Strip)`: Mint green with cute cartoon dinos and fluffy smiling clouds.
- **🎸 Rock Band & Music Frames**:
  - `Garage Rock Tour (1x4 Strip)`: Stage black with rock horns (🤘), electric guitar graphics, lightning bolts (⚡), amplifier dials, and VIP tour pass graphics.
  - `90s Grunge Cassette (1x3 Strip)`: Charcoal cassette casing with dual spools, magnetic tape window, and mixtape tracklist.
  - `Y2K Punk Rebel (2x2 Grid)`: Midnight gothic purple with holographic CD disks, flame decals, headphones, and spray stencils.
  - `Vinyl Record Groove (1x4 Strip)`: Concentric vinyl grooves, turntable center label, and 33 RPM stereo marks.
- **✨ Signature Classic Frames**:
  - `Handflair Signature White (1x3 Strip)` & `Signature Midnight Noir (2x2 Grid)`.

### 3. Guest vs. Member System
- **Guest Access**: Immediate shoot with free cartoon, band, and classic starter frames. Direct high-res download without mandatory login.
- **Member Access**: Unlocks all premium 2x2 grids, member-only cartoon & band editions, and persistent memory vault archiving (IndexedDB).

### 4. High-Res Canvas Rendering
- All cartoon doodles, smiling stars, bunny ears, cassette tapes, lightning bolts, and Handflair signature stamps are rendered directly onto the high-resolution 2x Retina canvas ready for sharp PNG & JPEG download!
